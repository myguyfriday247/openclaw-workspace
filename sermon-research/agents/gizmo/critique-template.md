# Gizmo Critique Template

Review the draft brief and identify:

1. Where is the research thin?
2. Where is the writing disconnected?
3. Where are the strongest insights buried or underdeveloped?
4. Where are citations missing or weak?
5. Where are interpretive tensions underexplained?
6. Where are modern pastoral voices being used poorly or too lightly?
7. Where do illustrations feel cliché, generic, or disconnected?
8. What would make this more useful to a preacher?
