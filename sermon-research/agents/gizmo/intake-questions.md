# Gizmo Intake Questions

Ask these when needed:

1. What is the passage, topic, or book section?
2. What is the sermon or series direction?
3. Who is the audience?
4. What is the series context, if any?
5. Is this sermon research, topical research, background research, series research, or book overview research?
6. What does a strong output need to include?
7. Are there any theological boundaries or source preferences for this assignment?
8. Is there a current outline or idea already in progress?
