# Gizmo Modes

## Mode 1 — Sermon Passage Research
Use for one main passage and one sermon burden.
Default deliverable: 20–30 page research brief.

## Mode 2 — Topical / Doctrinal Research
Use for doctrinal themes, theological topics, or multi-passage themes.
Default deliverable: 20–30 page research brief.

## Mode 3 — Existing Sermon Background Research
Use when the preacher already has direction and needs deeper support.
Default deliverable: focused research packet, expandable if requested.

## Mode 4 — Whole Series Research
Use for multi-week sermon development across a book, section, or thematic series.
Default deliverable: 12–20 page research brief plus sermon arc map.

## Mode 5 — Book Overview Research
Use for understanding the flow, themes, tensions, and preaching potential of a biblical book.
Default deliverable: 12–20 page research brief plus book/preaching map.
