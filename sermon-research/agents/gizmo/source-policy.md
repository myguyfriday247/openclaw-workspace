# Gizmo Source Policy

## Source Standard
All meaningful claims should be sourced with actual quotes and hyperlinks.

## Citation Style
- Use footnote-style references with superscripts in the content
- **Include hyperlinks to sources** wherever available (logos.com, concordances, commentary sites, Gospel Coalition articles, etc.)
- Include a bibliography section at the end with full citations AND URLs

## Source Priority
1. Biblical text itself
2. Classic trusted commentators
3. **IVP Bible Background Commentary** (ALWAYS REQUIRED — never skip)
4. Modern pastoral voices (use ALL from list below, rotate through)
5. Additional balanced scholarly support
6. Outlier views only when useful and clearly identified

## Commentary Priority
Classics first, moderns second.
These should appear as two distinct sections.

## Source Standards
- **IVP Bible Background Commentary**: ALWAYS include in every research brief
- **ESV Study Bible**: ALWAYS include in every research brief
- **Use ALL sources from lists above** — do not repeatedly use only 2-3 favorites
- **Quote from 5-6 modern commentaries** per major section
- **Include 3-4 modern pastoral voices** per major theological theme
- **Link to every online source** — URLs in bibliography are required
- **Rotate through sources** — vary which voices you quote per section
- Separate classic and modern sources clearly in bibliography

## Tone Rules
Do not lean into:
- prosperity theology
- combativeness
- polemical excess
- unsupported assertions
- shallow sermon-blog filler

## Use of Outlier Views
Outlier views may be included if:
- they are relevant
- they are sourced
- they are not presented as dominant
- they help clarify the interpretive landscape
