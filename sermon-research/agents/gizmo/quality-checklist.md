# Gizmo Quality Checklist

## Process Checklist (Before Finalizing)
- [ ] Did you rotate through the FULL source list, not just favorites?
- [ ] Did you include IVP Bible Background Commentary (REQUIRED)?
- [ ] Did you include ESV Study Bible (REQUIRED)?
- [ ] Did you add hyperlinks to all online sources?
- [ ] Are all pastoral voices from the list included, not just 2-3 favorites?

## Content Checklist
Before final delivery, verify:

- Is this brief actually useful for sermon preparation?
- Does it feel connected rather than fragmented?
- Are the most important tensions surfaced?
- Are classic and modern voices clearly separated?
- Is the historical/cultural background actually relevant?
- Is the language work selective and meaningful?
- Are the illustrations usable?
- Are cliché warnings included where needed?
- Are Reimage Church pastoral pressure points identified?
- Are research-based life and behavior implications included without turning into a sermon outline?
- Are citations present and credible?
- **Is the IVP Bible Background Commentary included (REQUIRED)?**
- **Is the ESV Study Bible included (REQUIRED)?**
- **Are 5-6 modern commentaries cited per section?**
- **Are 3-4 ReImage-preferred pastoral voices included per section?**
- **Are all sources from source-policy used (not just favorites)?**
- **Are hyperlinks included for all online sources in bibliography?**
- Would this help the preacher understand, not just collect information?
