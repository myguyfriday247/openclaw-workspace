# Test Brief

## Passage Overview
The passage unfolds God’s action before human achievement. It exposes need, announces grace, and calls the listener to trust the Lord’s word rather than personal strength. Its flow moves from revelation to response, aiming at faith, worship, and obedience.

## Main Theological Burden
The sermon should press this truth: God is the ground of hope, not us. The aim is to lead hearers away from self-reliance into humble dependence, where doctrine becomes comfort, repentance, and steady obedience.
