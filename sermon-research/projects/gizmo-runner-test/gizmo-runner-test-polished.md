

# Test Brief

## Passage Overview

The passage unfolds God's action before any human achievement. It exposes need, announces grace, and calls the listener to trust the Lord's word rather than personal strength. The movement is from revelation to response—aimed squarely at faith, worship, and obedience.

## Main Theological Burden

The sermon should press this truth: God is the ground of hope, not us. The aim is to lead hearers away from self-reliance and into humble dependence—where doctrine becomes comfort, repentance becomes possible, and obedience finds its steady footing.
