# Sample Gizmo Research Plan
## Judges 6 — God Is Peace

## 1. Assignment Summary
Research Judges 6 for a sermon in the series **God Is**, focused on the first-mention significance of **Yahweh-Shalom / The Lord Is Peace**, helping Reimage Church understand how knowing who God is shapes who we are and how we live as His image bearers.

## 2. Research Mode
Sermon passage research

## 3. Main Questions This Research Must Answer
1. What is happening in Judges 6 historically, spiritually, and literarily?
2. What does the divine name in Judges 6:24 actually mean?
3. How does “peace” function in the passage: emotional calm, covenant wholeness, divine assurance, or something broader?
4. How do classic commentators interpret Gideon’s fear, weakness, and calling?
5. How are modern pastors using this passage in “God is Peace” messaging, and where are they strong or thin?
6. What false assumptions about peace need to be corrected?
7. What implications for identity, trust, obedience, and discipleship naturally arise from the text?

## 4. Major Tensions / Risks
- reducing peace to emotional comfort
- disconnecting the divine name from the narrative context
- flattening Gideon into either hero or coward without nuance
- making application sentimental instead of text-driven
- missing the covenant and missional dimensions of peace

## 5. Proposed Sections for Final Brief
- passage overview
- historical and cultural context
- translation and language insights
- classic commentary synthesis
- modern pastoral voice synthesis
- interpretive tensions and objections
- cross references
- theological themes
- Reimage pastoral pressure points
- illustration paths
- pastoral framing summary
- research-based implications for life and behavior

## 6. Subagent Assignments
- Context Agent: Midianite oppression, Judges cycle, covenant backdrop, altar destruction context
- Language Agent: shalom, fear language, messenger of the Lord, selective translation comparisons
- Commentary Agent: classic and modern treatment of Gideon, divine calling, and the naming of the altar
- Theology Agent: peace as divine identity, peace and presence, peace and obedience, peace and covenant wholeness
- Objections Agent: fear, hesitation, “God can’t use me,” modern reduction of peace to inner calm
- Modern Voices Agent: current “God is Peace” preaching patterns and their strengths/weaknesses
- Illustration Agent: fear-to-assurance pathways, peace in chaos, false-peace contrasts, object lesson possibilities
- Cross-Reference Agent: primary and secondary biblical connections
- Series Arc Agent: not needed for this assignment

## 7. What This Brief Must Make Clear
The name “The Lord is Peace” is not mainly a slogan about calm feelings. In Judges 6 it emerges in a moment of fear, divine presence, calling, and covenant restoration. God’s peace is bound to who He is and to His assurance in the middle of obedience.

## 8. What a Weak Version Would Miss
- the narrative setting of the divine name
- the covenantal richness of peace
- the interpretive importance of Gideon’s fear
- the difference between true peace and sentimental comfort
- how the text speaks to obedience, identity, and trust
