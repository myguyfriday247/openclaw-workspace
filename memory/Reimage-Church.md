# Reimage Church - Complete Overview

## Core Identity
**Name:** Reimage Church (Greenville/Winterville, NC)
- **Pronunciation:** "RE-IMAGE" (not RAY MAGE)
- **Meaning:** To be imaged again - restoration to God's original design
- **Computer science analogy:** Like reinstalling an OS to fix corrupted software
- **Theological meaning:** God removes sin/brokenness and restores us to His original intent

---

## Mission Statement
**"Reach People, Grow Disciples, and Send Leaders"**

Or condensed: **Reach. Grow. Send.**

Based on Matthew 28:19-20 (The Great Commission):
- Go and make disciples of all nations
- Baptize them in the name of the Father, Son, and Holy Spirit
- Teach new disciples to obey all commands
- Jesus promises to be with us always

## Core Values (8 Total)

### 1. Pursue Diversity
We strive to be a community that reflects our city. The Gospel is hope for every generation, age, background, and nation—we seek to reach and do life with people who are not like us.

### 2. Experience God
God created us to exist in relationship with Him. Our rebellion separated us from His presence, but Jesus made a way for us to experience God's presence—He now rests inside us. We have access to Him anytime when we remove distractions. He is always present, waiting for us to run to Him.

### 3. Share the Gospel
The Gospel changes lives—it's the message we preach and the power to transform. We run toward the hurting, sick, oppressed, and broken with the only message that can make them whole and restored to God.

### 4. Demonstrate Growth
The sign of a true believer is fruit (John 15). We can't create fruit—it's a product of being connected to Jesus. Just as we can't save ourselves, we can't change by ourselves. We must surrender to the Holy Spirit, listen for His voice, and obey what He says.

### 5. Send Leaders
We will send out the leaders we raise up to carry the mission wherever there is opportunity. God has to hoard people but to train, equip, resource, and send leaders for the not placed us here Kingdom.

### 6. Seek the Kingdom First
Our lives are not our own—we've been bought with a great price. We generously give our time, energy, talents, and resources to see God's Kingdom advance both locally and globally.

### 7. Fight for Unity
A united people are unstoppable. Jesus said a united church would prove God's love. Unity requires being laser-focused on Jesus, setting aside the inconsequential, honoring one another, and submitting to each other. It's not easy but worth fighting for.

### 8. Prioritize Obedience
Jesus' teaching focuses on obeying Him, not just knowing Him. He said those who hear AND obey build their house on the rock. We measure maturity not by knowledge or time served, but by acts of obedience. When God speaks, we obey.

## Strategy: Gather. Grow. Go.

### GATHER — Bring People Together
Jesus called people to belong, not just believe. We bring people together into unity:
- Create space for God to move
- Unite across generations, backgrounds, cultures
- Invite others to encounter God's presence and love
- We gather for transformation, not attendance

### GROW — Look Back, Look Up, Look Forward
Intentional growth through three perspectives:

**Look Back (Reflection):**
- How have I been walking with Jesus?
- Am I living in obedience to what He's shown me?
- Where do I need prayer, support, or accountability?

**Look Up (Worship & Community):**
- Worship — respond to God with praise and surrender
- Fellowship — form and deepen relationships
- Discipleship — shaped by Scripture and spiritual practices
- Ministry to one another — love, serve, build each other up

**Look Forward (Direction):**
- What's the next step God is leading me to take?
- Who can I serve, reach, or encourage this week?
- How can I put what I've learned into action?

### GO — Live on Mission
We are the church—sent to make a difference wherever we live, work, and play:
- Obedience — put Jesus' teachings into practice
- Mission — live out faith in everyday life
- Multiplication — help others know Jesus
