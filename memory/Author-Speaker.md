# Author & Speaker Work - Simple Disciple

## Overview
Chris Hopkins is developing "The Simple Disciple" as both a book and podcast, focused on simplifying discipleship into clear, actionable steps. The core thesis: **"Following Jesus is simple. Not easy, but simple."**

## From the Author's Story (Personal Journey)

**Background:**
- 35+ years following Jesus
- Nearly 20 years in full-time ministry
- Oldest son, independent, achiever personality
- Carried achievement into faith—tried to be "best Christian"

**The Struggle:**
- Tried hard, failed often
- Asked: "If God is all-powerful and good, why isn't this easier?"
- Core problem: Came to Jesus for redemption, then tried to live the Christian life without Him
- Drive and achievement that drew him to ministry became the place he struggled most
- "For decades, I worked hard for God. Sometimes there were wins. More often, there was weariness."

**The Turning Point (5 years ago):**
- Realized he was "powering through life and asking God to bless my plan"
- God showed him: The problem wasn't Jesus' way was complicated—it was asking Jesus to follow HIM
- Repented and yoked life to Jesus
- Began practicing: Hear Jesus and do what He says—then ask again
- Simple. Not easy. Empowered by His Spirit.

## Author's Personal Illustrations (Chapter 3 - A Clear Gospel)

**Seven Problematic Versions of Christianity:**

1. **Jesus + Blessings** (Taylor)
   - Prays hardest when life jams
   - Measures God's goodness by prayer results
   - Faith surges with good outcomes, stalls with bad
   - Soul rides circumstances like waves

2. **Jesus + Traditions** (Morgan)
   - Sundays on autopilot
   - Knows when to stand and sit
   - Bible is familiar, not fresh
   - Near church things, but unsure if near Christ

3. **Jesus + Merit** (Avery - the author's story)
   - Spiritual dashboard: chapters read, minutes prayed, hours volunteered
   - Numbers rise = relief; numbers dip = shame
   - Compliments are oxygen; criticism confirms failure
   - Obedience tries to earn love instead of responding to it

4. **Jesus + Appearance** (Jordan)
   - Flawless aesthetic, winsome faith
   - Admires Jesus, quotes Him, posts #JesusIsAwesome
   - Edits Jesus when His words cross preferences or cost pride
   - Goal: be liked, not transformed

5. **Jesus + ...** (additional versions in progress)

## Key Discipleship Concepts

### The "Ask. Listen. Obey. Repeat." Rhythm
Core practice: hear Jesus and do what He says—then ask again.

### The Problem with "Jesus +"
Many versions of Christianity add something to Jesus:
- Jesus + Blessings (results-based faith)
- Jesus + Traditions (rut-based faith)
- Jesus + Merit (performance-based faith)
- Jesus + Appearance (image-based faith)

These create weariness and produce little fruit.

### The Gospel Core
- Sin = switching sources (choosing self over God as Source)
- We were created to live FROM God and WITH God
- The gospel reconnects us to the Vine (Jesus)
- Life flows from that connection; death follows separation

### Obedience Principle
- "If you love Me, you will obey My commands"
- Obedience as love response, not legalism
- God empowers what He commands

### The "Self Battery" Problem
- Humanity tries to run life on "Self battery"
- It was never built to hold that charge
- Connection to Jesus = recharging
