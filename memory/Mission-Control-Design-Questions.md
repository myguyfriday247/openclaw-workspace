# Mission Control Design Questions

A rotating set of questions to help design how Mission Control works for Chris.

---

## Workflow & Daily Rhythm

1. What's your ideal morning routine? When do you first check messages?
2. What time of day do you feel most creative? Most productive?
3. How do you prefer to capture ideas as they come to you?
4. What's one task you wish happened automatically each day?

## What's Working / Not Working

5. What's been most helpful so far in how we work together?
6. What's felt like noise or friction?
7. What's one thing I do that annoys you?
8. When I complete a task ahead of schedule, do you like being surprised, or would you prefer I wait?

## Project & Task Management

9. How do you track things you need to do? (Notion, notes app, email, memory?)
10. When you have a new project, how do you like to break it down?
11. What's your tolerance for seeing "empty" space vs. wanting things filled in?

## Communication Preferences

12. How detailed do you want my responses to be?
    - **Answer (Feb 5):** "I care more that they are clear than detailed."

13. When should I interrupt you vs. wait for you to come to me?
    - **Answer (Feb 5):** "Interrupt me if you are working on something and need direction to complete the task you are working on or have questions."

14. What's the best way for you to receive feedback or suggestions?
    - **Answer (Feb 5):** Could be added to the daily Mission Control cron (the 10 AM questions).

## Mission Control Specific

15. What would make you visit Mission Control every day?
16. What would you want to see at a glance when you open it?
17. Should projects appear automatically as we work on them, or do you want to add them yourself?
18. What other "tabs" would be useful? (Calendar? Notes? Links? Stats?)
19. How do you want to add new tasks — type them in Mission Control, or tell me and I add them?
20. Would you like me to summarize our work each evening for the next day?

## Fun / Personal

21. What's the one thing I should always remind you about?
22. Is there a recurring meeting or commitment I should help you prepare for?
23. What does a "successful week" look like for you?

---

## Rotation Strategy

Send 5-7 questions per day, rotating through categories:
- Monday: Workflow & Rhythm
- Tuesday: What's Working / Not Working  
- Wednesday: Project & Task Management
- Thursday: Communication Preferences
- Friday: Mission Control Specific
- Weekend: Fun / Personal + catch-up

## 💡 Idea for Something I Could Build for You

**Sermon Prep Assistant**

You spend hours preparing sermons. What if I could help?

Here's how it would work:

1. **You tell me the passage** you're preaching on (like "John 3" or "Ephesians 5")

2. **I search for you:**
   - What other pastors have taught on this passage
   - Commentary insights
   - Related sermons and series
   - Illustrations other people used

3. **I organize it for you:**
   - Main themes in the text
   - 3-5 key points people have made
   - Helpful quotes and stories you could borrow
   - Questions your audience might ask

4. **I remember what you've preached before** so I don't suggest the same ideas twice.

**Why this helps:**
- You spend less time searching, more time thinking
- You get fresh ideas from other preachers
- It's like having a research assistant who never sleeps

Think this sounds useful? Or is there something else you'd rather I build?
