Week 3: God Says I Am Sent

Series: Defined - Who God Says You Are | Date: April 26, 2026

Key Passages: Matthew 5:13-16, John 15:5, James 2:17

INTRODUCTION & BURDEN

When identity and purpose take root, action follows. The Christian life is not passive—it's active. We are called to live as servants, witnesses, and disciples who embody the message of Jesus in real, tangible ways.

This doesn't mean striving harder—it means abiding deeper. As we remain connected to Christ, our lives begin to produce fruit. Our actions—how we love, serve, speak, and live—become a visible expression of an invisible transformation.

The burden: To help people understand that true faith produces visible action, and that fruit comes from connection with Christ, not just Christian activity.

SECTION 1: THEOLOGICAL FOUNDATION

1.1 Faith That Lives

The book of James addresses a different problem: dead orthodoxy.

"Faith by itself, if it does not have works, is dead." — James 2:17

The key distinction: James is not saying we are saved by works. James is saying real faith produces works. Saving faith is never alone.

1.2 The Vine and the Branches

"I am the vine; you are the branches. If you remain in me and I in you, you will bear much fruit; apart from me you can do nothing." — John 15:5
  • Christ is the vine (the source)
  • We are the branches (the channel)
  • The Father is the gardener (the sustainer)

1.3 Salt and Light

Matthew 5:13-16 is part of the Sermon on the Mount.

Salt's purpose: Preserves from decay, adds flavor, creates thirst.

Light's purpose: Reveals truth, guides in darkness, points the way.

SECTION 2: BIBLICAL CONTEXT

2.1 Matthew 5:13-16 - Salt and Light

Salt: "You are the salt of the earth. But if the salt loses its saltiness, how can it be made salty again?"

Light: "You are the light of the world. A town built on a hill cannot be hidden... Let your light shine before others, that they may see your good deeds and glorify your Father in heaven."

2.2 John 15:5 - Abiding

The dependency: We cannot produce fruit on our own. Everything good comes from Christ.

The connection: Remaining = fellowship. Fruit = evidence of life.

2.3 James 2:17 - Faith Working

The demonstration: Real faith produces works. Works don't save us, but they evidence salvation.

SECTION 3: THE IMPORTANCE OF ACTION

3.1 Faith Is Meant to Be Lived

The problem with passive faith: "I believe in my heart." "I don't need to do anything."

The call: Faith is trust that leads to action. Faith obeys (John 14:15). Faith follows (Matthew 4:20).

3.2 You Are the Light in Darkness

The contrast: Darkness = sin, error, deception. Light = truth, righteousness, clarity.

Living as light: Don't hide your faith. Live visibly for Christ. Let your good deeds be seen.

3.3 Fruit Comes from Connection

The distinction: Working for God vs. abiding in God. Busy for God vs. connected to God.

The danger: We can be busy without being fruitful. We can do Christian things without knowing Christ.

SECTION 4: LIVING SENT

4.1 The Sending Paradigm

"As the Father has sent me, so I am sending you." — John 20:21

Being sent means: Carrying Christ's authority, speaking Christ's message, doing Christ's work, representing Christ's kingdom.

4.2 The Scope of Mission

The myth: Mission is for missionaries overseas. Only "full-time Christian workers" do mission.

The reality: Every believer is sent. Every context is mission field.

4.3 Faith in Action

  • Care for widows and orphans (James 1:27)
  • Don't show favoritism (James 2:1-9)
  • Control the tongue (James 3:1-12)

SECTION 5: THE FRUIT OF ABIDING

5.1 What Is Fruit?

Spiritual fruit: Love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, gentleness, self-control (Galatians 5:22).

Other fruit: New believers (Romans 1:13), Good works (Colossians 1:10), Praise (Hebrews 13:15).

5.2 How Fruit Grows

The process: Connection to the vine, spiritual nourishment, pruning (John 15:2), time and growth.

The assurance: If you abide, you will bear fruit. It's not about trying harder. It's about staying connected.

SECTION 6: COMMON QUESTIONS

"Doesn't this contradict salvation by faith?" No. James is not saying we are saved by works. He is saying that saving faith produces works.

"How do I know if I'm abiding?" Do you have spiritual fruit? Is your life changing? Are you growing in love?

"What if I try and fail?" The call is to abide, not to perform perfectly. Your job is to remain.

SECTION 7: ILLUSTRATIONS

7.1 The Branch and the Vine

A branch doesn't try to produce fruit. It simply stays connected to the vine, and the vine produces fruit through it.

7.2 The Lamp on a Stand

A lamp doesn't hide its light. It puts the light on display so everyone can see.

7.3 The Apple Tree

An apple tree doesn't try to produce apples—it simply is an apple tree, and apples come naturally.

SECTION 8: KEY QUOTES

"The Christian life is not about trying harder to be good. It's about letting Christ live His life through us." — Tim Keller
Tim Keller
"Faith that doesn't change your life isn't faith at all." — J.D. Greear

SECTION 9: CONCLUSION

When identity and purpose take root, action follows. The Christian life is not passive—it's active. This doesn't mean striving harder—it means abiding deeper.

The invitation: Stop striving. Start abiding. Let your life speak.
