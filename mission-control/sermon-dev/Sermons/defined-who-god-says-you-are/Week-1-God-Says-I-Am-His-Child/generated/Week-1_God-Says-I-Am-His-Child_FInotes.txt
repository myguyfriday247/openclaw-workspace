# Defined — Who God Says You Are
# Week 1: God Says I Am His Child
Key Verse: Romans 8:16 — "The Spirit himself bears witness with our spirit that we are children of God."

------
**1. Adoption, Not Just Forgiveness**
Forgiveness clears a debt. Adoption gives a name, an inheritance, a family. God didn't just say "we're even" — He said "you're family." Before the foundation of the world, He chose you and placed you in His family.

**Reflection:**
What would change if you stopped trying to earn God's love and started living as His child?

**Application:**
This week, when you pray, use the word "Father" instead of "God" or "Lord." Notice how the intimacy changes your prayer.

____

------
**2. The Orphan Voice Is Lying to You**
The orphan always feels like they have to prove themselves. A child knows they belong. If your worth depends on performance — prayer enough, Bible reading enough, serving enough — you're living like an orphan.

**Reflection:**
When does the orphan voice speak loudest in your life? When do you feel like you have to prove yourself to God?

**Application:**
Pay attention this week when the orphan voice shows up. Name it: "That's the orphan voice." Write down three truths about who you are in Christ and put them where you'll see them daily.

____

------
**3. Your Identity Creates Your Obedience**
Most of us try to change behavior to earn identity. But it works the other way around. When you know whose you are, you know who you are — and obedience flows from belonging, not obligation.

**Reflection:**
Are you performing for acceptance, or living from the security of belonging?

**Application:**
This week, ask a different question: "What is my Dad doing that I get to be part of?" Look for what God is doing around you and join in.

____

------
**Final Reflection:**
Who am I — an orphan trying to earn, or a child already home?
What would change if I truly believed nothing I do can change my identity?
How will I live differently this week from my identity, not for my identity?
