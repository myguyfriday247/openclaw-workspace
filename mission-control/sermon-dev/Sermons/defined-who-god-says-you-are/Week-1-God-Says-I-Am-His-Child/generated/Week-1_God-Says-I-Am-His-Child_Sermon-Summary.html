God Says I Am His Child

Series: Defined — Who God Says You Are | Week 1

Key Verse: Romans 8:16 — "The Spirit himself bears witness with our spirit that we are children of God."

Summary

Many Christians live with the constant feeling that they have to earn God's love. They pray more, read their Bible more, serve more—always striving, always wondering if they've done enough. But Pastor Chris reveals that this "orphan mindset" is Counter to the gospel. God didn't just forgive us—He adopted us. Before the foundation of the world, He chose us, placed us in His family, and gave us His name and inheritance. When you understand your identity as God's child, everything changes. You don't perform for acceptance—you live from the security of belonging.

The enemy uses the orphan voice to keep us performing, but the Spirit testifies to our true identity. The next time you feel like you have to prove yourself to God, remember: the verdict is already in. You are God's child—not because of what you've done, but because of what Christ has done. Stop performing for identity. Start living from it.
