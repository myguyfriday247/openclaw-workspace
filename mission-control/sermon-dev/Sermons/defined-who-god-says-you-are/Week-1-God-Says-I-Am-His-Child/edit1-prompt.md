EDIT 1 NOTES FROM CHRIS:

1. For the illustration of the self-made child… I want to start with "we are influenced by our culture so much. And our culture is all about the self-made person. I'm a self-made millionaire, no your daddy gave you his company. I'm a self-taught superstar. No someone trained you. Someone made YouTube videos, someone wrote a book you read. I'm self-made. But…never self-made child."

2. When we get to the practical next steps for all points, I want it to feel like suggestions, maybe god wants you to do this week, maybe god wants this. It is about getting people to listen to god, not do a specific task. These are ideas that they may use, but we are encouraging people to think/listen.

3. For point 2. I want to make the same point but stay in Romans 8 first. I would ask "so how many of you were alive when Jesus was on earth?" Well God gave his son before you were born. Before you did anything good or bad. I would then mention Ephesians 1:4-5 and continue on the foundation of the world theme. But want to tie the point to romans 8 first.

4. For the unconditional gift illustration. I want the illustration to be more focused on the person receiving has to try to earn it. Not the giver requires them to earn it.

5. For the conclusion illustration. Instead of the orphan on the porch illustration I want to talk about where I am at in my life. I do a lot of things because I'm supposed to. I do them almost out of obligation. I share the gospel, because that's what we do. We've been saved, we've been redeemed, we've been rescued. That's what we should do. The problem is, because I am a child of God, I want it to be who I am. Not doing it out of service or even out of love/gratitude. But because it's who I am. That's what a son does. He does what His father does because its who his father has raised him to be.
