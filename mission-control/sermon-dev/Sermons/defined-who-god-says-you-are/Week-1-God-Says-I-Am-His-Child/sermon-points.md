# Week 1: God Says I Am His Child - Sermon Points

**Status:** ✅ APPROVED  
**Date Approved:** March 24, 2026  
**Central Passage:** Romans 8:1-17

---

## Point 1: Adoption, Not Just Forgiveness

**Core Truth:** God doesn't just save us FROM sin — He brings us INTO His family.

**Key Scripture:**
- John 1:12 — "But to all who did receive him, who believed in his name, he gave the right to become children of God."

**Main Idea:** Forgiveness deals with guilt; adoption deals with identity. God offers us more than forgiveness — He offers us family.

---

## Point 2: Chosen Before We Were Worthy

**Core Truth:** Our adoption was planned before time — based on God's will, not our worth.

**Key Scripture:**
- Ephesians 1:4-5 — "Even as he chose us in him before the foundation of the world, that we should be holy and blameless before him. In love he predestined us for adoption to himself as sons, through Jesus Christ, according to the purpose of his will."

**Main Idea:** We didn't earn our adoption, we couldn't earn it, and we can't lose it. God chose us before we were worthy.

---

## Point 3: The Spiritual Orphan Mindset

**Core Truth:** Many Christians live as "spiritual orphans" — striving for approval, afraid of failure.

**Key Points:**
- **Symptoms of Spiritual Orphanhood:**
  - Performance-based identity (worth based on what I do)
  - Fear of rejection (people-pleasing to secure acceptance)
  - Spiritual insecurity (not sure God really loves me)
  - Comparison and envy (measuring against others)
  - Lack of intimacy (prayer feels like reporting)

- **The Lie:** "I must earn what I already have"

**Main Idea:** The orphan spirit produces people who are always striving, always performing, always trying to earn what they already have been given.

---

## Point 5: Living From Our Identity

**Core Truth:** When we know whose we are, we know who we are.

**Key Implications:**
- We can face failure differently: Our identity is secure in Christ
- We can receive correction differently: Discipline is from a loving Father
- We can relate to others differently: We don't need to compare or compete
- We can face trials differently: Our security doesn't depend on circumstances

**Main Idea:** Stop performing for identity. Start living FROM it.

---

## Closing Invitation

"The invitation this week is simple: Stop performing for identity. Start living from it. You are God's beloved child — not because of what you've done, but because of what Christ has done."
