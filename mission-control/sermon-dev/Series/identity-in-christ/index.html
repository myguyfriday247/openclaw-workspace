SERIES SUMMARY

Everyone is searching for identity. Some look to success, others to relationships, others to their past or their pain. Culture tells us to define ourselves—to create who we want to be. But the gospel offers something far better: a new identity that is received, not achieved.

In this series, we explore four foundational dimensions: who we are, what we belong to, how we live, and what God produces in us.

WEEK 1: IDENTITY — GOD SAYS I AM HIS CHILD

Key Passages

John 1:12 | Ephesians 1:4-5 | 2 Corinthians 5:17

"Therefore, if anyone is in Christ, he is a new creation. The old has passed away; behold, the new has come." — 2 Corinthians 5:17

Outline

1. Truth: Identity is Received, Not Achieved

We don't earn sonship—we are given it through Christ.

Application: Stop defining yourself by performance or past failure.

Next Step: Where am I still trying to prove myself instead of receiving God's love?

2. Truth: You Are Fully Known and Fully Loved

God chose you knowing everything about you.

Application: Bring your whole self to God, not just your best parts.

Next Step: What part of my life am I still hiding from God?

3. Truth: Identity Shapes Everything Else

What you believe about yourself determines how you live.

Application: Begin replacing lies with truth daily.

Next Step: What lie about my identity do I need to replace with God's truth?
