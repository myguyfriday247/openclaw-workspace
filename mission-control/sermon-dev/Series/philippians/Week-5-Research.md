# Week 5 — Philippians 3:1–21

## What Really Matters

### Key Passage
> "More than that, I count all things as loss because of the surpassing worth of knowing Christ Jesus my Lord." — Philippians 3:8

### Research Summary

**1. Credentials Don't Save**
- "If anyone else has a mind to put confidence in the flesh, I far more" (v. 4)
- Paul had: circumcision, tribe of Benjamin, Hebrew of Hebrews,Pharisee, righteousness under law
- "But whatever gain I had, I counted as loss for the sake of Christ" (v. 7)
- External markers cannot bring you closer to God

**2. Knowing Christ Is Participation**
- "That I may know Him and the power of His resurrection and the fellowship of His suffering" (v. 10)
- Not information — experiential
- "being conformed to His death" (v. 10)
- Transforms how you live

**3. Pressing Forward**
- "Forgetting what is behind and straining toward what is ahead, I press on toward the goal" (v. 13-14)
- "I press on toward the goal for the prize of the upward call of God in Christ Jesus" (v. 14)
- The Christian life is a forward pursuit, not a finished achievement
- Source: "Forgetting what is behind and straining toward what is ahead"
