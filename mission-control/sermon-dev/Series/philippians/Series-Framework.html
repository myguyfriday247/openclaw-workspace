Philippians Series Framework

6 Weeks · August 2 - September 6, 2026
← Back to Series
1
Philippians 1:1–11 — The Kind of Life That Produces Joy
Observation 1: Joy is rooted in shared gospel purpose, not shared preferences.
Observation 2: Confidence in people grows when you trust God's work in them.
Observation 3: Spiritual maturity is measured by growing, discerning love.
2
Philippians 1:12–30 — When Life Doesn't Go Your Way
Observation 1: What feels like a setback may actually be strategic for the gospel.
Observation 2: Pressure reveals what you truly value.
Observation 3: Courage in opposition is a visible sign of deeper reality.
3
Philippians 2:1–11 — A Different Way to Live
Observation 1: Unity requires intentional selflessness, not passive agreement.
Observation 2: Jesus redefines greatness by refusing to grasp for status.
Observation 3: God exalts what the world overlooks.
4
Philippians 2:12–30 — God Is Working in You
Observation 1: You are responsible to live out what God has already worked in.
Observation 2: God fuels both your desire and your ability to follow Him.
Observation 3: A faithful life shines most clearly in a dark environment.
5
Philippians 3:1–21 — What Really Matters
Observation 1: You can have all the right credentials and still miss what matters most.
Observation 2: Knowing Christ is not information—it is participation.
Observation 3: Spiritual growth requires a forward focus.
6
Philippians 4:1–23 — A Life That Stands Firm
Observation 1: Stability in life is deeply connected to unity in relationships.
Observation 2: Peace is experienced when trust replaces anxiety.
Observation 3: Contentment is learned by depending on Christ in every situation.

🔑 Final Thread — Each Sermon Builds on the Last

  • Week 1: Where joy comes from
  • Week 2: What happens when it's tested
  • Week 3: The mindset that sustains it
  • Week 4: The power that enables it
  • Week 5: The focus that protects it
  • Week 6: The stability that results from it
