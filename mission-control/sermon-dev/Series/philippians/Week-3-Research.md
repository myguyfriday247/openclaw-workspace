# Week 3 — Philippians 2:1–11

## A Different Way to Live

### Key Passage
> "Let this mind be in you which was also in Christ Jesus." — Philippians 2:5

### Research Summary

**1. Unity Through Humility**
- "Do nothing from selfish ambition or conceit, but in humility consider others better than yourselves" (v. 3)
- Unity is not passive — it's active self-denial
- "Look not only to your own interests, but also to the interests of others" (v. 4)

**2. The Christ-Hymn (Kenosis)**
- "Who, although He existed in the form of God, did not regard equality with God a thing to be grasped" (v. 6)
- "But emptied Himself, taking the form of a bond-servant" (v. 7)
- "He humbled Himself and became obedient unto death — even death on a cross" (v. 8)

**3. Exaltation Through Humility**
- "Therefore God highly exalted Him... that at the name of Jesus every knee should bow" (v. 9-11)
- Pattern: Lower → Higher
- What the world overlooks, God lifts up
- Source: "Because He humbled Himself, God highly exalted Him"
