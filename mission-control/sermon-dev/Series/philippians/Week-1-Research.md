# Week 1 — Philippians 1:1–11

## The Kind of Life That Produces Joy

### Key Passage
> "because of your partnership in the gospel from the first day until now." — Philippians 1:5

### Research Summary

**1. Joy Through Partnership**
- Paul thanks God "with joy" for their partnership (v. 3-4)
- The partnership is financial (fellowship) but also gospel-centered
- Source: Don Carson, The Gospel Coalition — "Partnership in the gospel from the first day until now"

**2. Confidence Anchored in God**
- Paul is confident "that He who began a good work in you will carry it on to completion" (v. 6)
- Confidence is in God's work, not human performance
- This allows patience with slow growth in others

**3. Discerning Love**
- Prays their love "abound more and more in knowledge and all discernment" (v. 9)
- Love shaped by knowledge, not blind loyalty
- Produces fruit: "pure, blameless, full of the fruit of righteousness" (v. 11)
