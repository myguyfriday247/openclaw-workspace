# Philippians — Big Idea & Sermon Map

**Series:** August 2 - September 6, 2026
**Weeks:** 6

---

## 🧭 Series Big Idea

When your life is anchored in Christ, you can have joy, humility, courage, and peace—no matter what happens around you.

---

## Week 1 — Philippians 1:1–11

### Title: The Kind of Life That Produces Joy

**🎣 Hook**
> "Most people think joy comes from what's happening to them. Paul says joy comes from what you're connected to."

**💡 Big Idea**
> Joy is produced through gospel-centered relationships and shared mission, not ideal circumstances.

**🔁 Transition to Week 2**
> "But what happens when your circumstances don't just get difficult… what happens when they get really difficult?"

---

## Week 2 — Philippians 1:12–30

### Title: When Life Doesn't Go Your Way

**🎣 Hook**
> "Some of the things you think are holding you back… are actually the very things God is using to move things forward."

**💡 Big Idea**
> God uses pressure, opposition, and even suffering to advance the gospel and deepen your faith.

**🔁 Transition to Week 3**
> "So how do you actually live like that? How do you stay steady when everything around you is shaking? Paul says—it starts with your mindset."

---

## Week 3 — Philippians 2:1–11

### Title: A Different Way to Live

**🎣 Hook**
> "The world says: climb, prove, win. Jesus says: lower, serve, surrender."

**💡 Big Idea**
> The mindset of Christ—humility over self-promotion—is the foundation for unity and true greatness.

**🔁 Transition to Week 4**
> "That sounds powerful… but also impossible. How do you actually live that out in real life?"

---

## Week 4 — Philippians 2:12–30

### Title: God Is Working in You

**🎣 Hook**
> "What if the pressure you feel to change… is actually proof that God is already working inside you?"

**💡 Big Idea**
> You can live this life because God is actively working in you—shaping both your desires and your actions.

**🔁 Transition to Week 5**
> "But there's still a tension… What do you do with everything you've built your life on up to this point?"

---

## Week 5 — Philippians 3:1–21

### Title: What Really Matters

**🎣 Hook**
> "What if the things you've spent your life trying to gain… are actually the things holding you back?"

**💡 Big Idea**
> Knowing Christ is of greater value than anything else, and the Christian life is a forward pursuit—not a finished achievement.

**🔁 Transition to Week 6**
> "So if Christ is everything… what does it actually look like to live that out day to day?"

---

## Week 6 — Philippians 4:1–23

### Title: A Life That Stands Firm

**🎣 Hook**
> "Peace isn't found when life calms down. Peace is found when your life is anchored to the right thing."

**💡 Big Idea**
> A life anchored in Christ produces peace, contentment, and stability in every situation.

**🔄 Closing the Series (Full Circle Moment)**
> We started this series talking about joy. Not surface-level happiness… but something deeper. And now we see it clearly— Joy comes from a life that is fully anchored in Christ.

**🔥 Optional Final Landing Line (This Will Preach)**
> When your life is anchored in Christ… circumstances don't control you, people don't define you, and fear doesn't stop you.
