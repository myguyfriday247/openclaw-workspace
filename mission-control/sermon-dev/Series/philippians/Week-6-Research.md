# Week 6 — Philippians 4:1–23

## A Life That Stands Firm

### Key Passage
> "I can do all things through Christ who strengthens me." — Philippians 4:13

### Research Summary

**1. Stability Through Unity**
- "I urge Euodia and I urge Syntyche to be of the same mind in the Lord" (v. 2)
- Addresses conflict directly — unresolved tension destabilizes everything
- "Stand firm in the Lord" (v. 1)
- Peace with God should lead to peace with others

**2. Peace Replaces Anxiety**
- "Be anxious for nothing, but in everything by prayer and petition, with thanksgiving, present your requests to God" (v. 6)
- Not dismiss anxiety — redirect it
- "And the peace of God, which transcends all understanding, will guard your hearts" (v. 7)
- Prayer → Peace pathway

**3. Contentment Is Learned**
- "I have learned to be content in whatever circumstances" (v. 11)
- "I can do all things through Christ who strengthens me" (v. 13)
- Paul experienced plenty and lack — neither defined him
- Contentment is developed, not natural
