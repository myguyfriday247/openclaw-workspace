# Week 4 — Philippians 2:12–30

## God Is Working in You

### Key Passage
> "Work out your own salvation with fear and trembling; for it is God who is at work in you." — Philippians 2:12-13

### Research Summary

**1. Work Out What God Worked In**
- "Work out your salvation" — not earn it, but express it
- Bring to surface what God has placed inside
- Faith is not passive — shows up in action
- Source: "work out your own salvation with fear and trembling"

**2. God Fuels Both Desire and Action**
- "For it is God who is at work in you, both to will and to work for His good pleasure" (v. 13)
- Not passive — God shapes what you want AND enables what you do
- Removes pride ("I'm doing this myself") and despair ("I can't do this")

**3. Shine as Lights**
- "Do all things without complaining or arguing, so that you may become blameless and pure" (v. 14-15)
- "Children of God without fault in the midst of a crooked and perverse generation" (v. 15)
- Lives become visible evidence — the contrast makes the witness visible
- "Shine as lights in the world" (v. 15)
