📅 Semester Planning Process

How we plan semesters on an ongoing, repeatable basis

1. Overview

Semester Planning is the process of determining what teaching series the church should consider over the next semester. It uses the 5-Fold Lens (Ephesians 4) and Five Discipleship Buckets to discern what kind of leadership the pulpit needs right now.

Important: This is NOT a committed plan. This is a "radar list" — series ideas to consider based on where our church is right now. Some may be preached next semester, some may not. The goal is to identify "sermons that should be done" and feed them into sermon-dev's "Upcoming Series" list at various development levels.

2. Stages

Stage 1
Church Needs Assessment

Discern what kind of leadership the church needs from the pulpit right now using the 5-Fold Lens.

Stage 2
Discipleship Bucket Selection

Identify which formation area (bucket) needs emphasis next based on the assessment.

Stage 3
Series Ideas & Sequencing

Brainstorm and order series ideas for consideration. Include space for my input.

Stage 4
Semester Documentation

Create the semester document listing series ideas to consider.

3. Tasks Per Stage

Stage 1: Church Needs Assessment

TaskWho
Review recent sermon feedback and attendance patternsYou
Walk through the 5-Fold Lens: Pastor, Prophet, Teacher, Evangelist, ApostleBoth
Document what each lens surfaces as current needsMe
Discuss and prioritize the most pressing needsBoth

Stage 2: Discipleship Bucket Selection

TaskWho
Present the Five Discipleship Buckets optionsMe
Explain each bucket: Bible/Self-Feeding, Spiritual Disciplines, Application/Felt Needs, Doctrine/Worldview, Missional LivingMe
Determine which bucket aligns with the Stage 1 assessmentBoth
Decide if series will be Expository, Topical, or HybridBoth

Stage 3: Series Ideas & Sequencing

TaskWho
Brainstorm potential series ideas for considerationBoth
I offer input and suggestions based on the buckets/lensesMe
Select series ideas to put on the radarYou
Order series ideas by priority/expediencyBoth
Note which ideas might span multiple semesters (e.g., book of the Bible)Me

Stage 4: Semester Documentation

TaskWho
Create semester document (HTML) with date rangeMe
List series ideas to consider (not commitments)Both
Include bucket focus and strategy notes for each ideaMe
You approve final semester radar documentYou
Add approved series to sermon-dev "Upcoming Series" listMe

5. Inputs / Outputs

📥 Stage 1 Inputs

  • Recent sermon feedback
  • Attendance and engagement observations
  • Knowledge of current church circumstances

📤 Stage 1 Outputs

  • 5-Fold Lens Assessment document
  • Prioritized list of church needs

📥 Stage 2 Inputs

  • 5-Fold Lens Assessment
  • Church needs priorities

📤 Stage 2 Outputs

  • Selected primary bucket
  • Selected strategy (Expository/Topical/Hybrid)

📥 Stage 3 Inputs

  • Selected bucket and strategy
  • My input and suggestions

📤 Stage 3 Outputs

  • Ordered list of series ideas to consider
  • Notes on multi-semester possibilities

📥 Stage 4 Inputs

  • Series ideas list
  • Bucket/strategy notes

📤 Stage 4 Outputs

  • Semester Radar Document (HTML)
  • Updated sermon-dev "Upcoming Series" list

6. Planning Schedule

Semester planning projects are triggered on:

  • January 1 — Summer YYYY Planning
  • May 1 — Fall XXXX Planning
  • September 1 — Spring YYYY Planning

This ensures we complete semester planning before the information is needed for the next semester.
