📚 Series Planning Process

How we plan individual teaching series

1. Overview

Series Planning takes a confirmed series from the semester plan and develops it into a fully scoped teaching series. This includes crafting the vision, framing, and the critical Series Teaching Map that holds the entire series together.

Goal: Create a clear, disciple-forming series architecture with a burden statement, big idea, arc, and week-by-week teaching map—before sermon development begins.

When this happens: After a series is confirmed in Semester Planning, but before any individual sermon work begins. This ensures every sermon has a clear backbone holding it to the series purpose.

2. Stages

Stage 0
Church Research

Research how other prominent churches have approached this type of series to inform our framing and structure.

Stage 1
Series Vision & Burden

Craft the internal burden statement that captures why this series exists.

Stage 2
Series Big Idea & Framing

Create the public-facing one-sentence big idea and determine series strategy.

Stage 3
Series Arc Design

Map how the series moves week to week and what the overall journey looks like.

Stage 4
Week-by-Week Map

Create the detailed teaching map showing each week's passage, focus, and contribution to the series.

Stage 5
Series Teaching Map Finalization

Compile all elements into the final Series Teaching Map document and get approval.

5. Inputs / Outputs

📥 Stage 0 (Church Research) Inputs

  • Confirmed series from Semester Planning
  • Series topic/title

📤 Stage 0 (Church Research) Outputs

  • Church Research Document with links
  • List of churches that taught similar series
  • Their framing, titles, and week count
  • Links for further exploration

📥 Stage 1 Inputs

  • Confirmed series from Semester Planning
  • Series topic/title
  • Target book of the Bible or topic
  • Estimated number of weeks
  • Discipleship bucket focus
  • Church Research findings

📤 Stage 1 Outputs

  • Series Burden Statement

📥 Stage 2 Inputs

  • Approved Burden Statement
  • Discipleship bucket and strategy context

📤 Stage 2 Outputs

  • Series Big Idea
  • Strategy statement (Expository/Topical/Hybrid + why)

📥 Stage 3 Inputs

  • Approved Burden and Big Idea
  • Number of weeks in series

📤 Stage 3 Outputs

  • Series Arc description

📥 Stage 4 Inputs

  • Approved Arc
  • Target passages (if known)

📤 Stage 4 Outputs

  • Week-by-Week Map (passage, focus, obedience, share)

📥 Stage 5 Inputs

  • All completed stages 1-4

📤 Stage 5 Outputs

  • Complete Series Teaching Map (1-2 page document)
  • Series ready for sermon development
