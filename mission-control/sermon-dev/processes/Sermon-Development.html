🎙️ Sermon Development Process

How we develop individual sermons week to week (11 stages)

1. Overview

Sermon Development takes a confirmed week from the Series Teaching Map and develops it into a finalized manuscript ready for delivery. This follows the fixed 11-stage process below.

Goal: Create a disciple-forming sermon that follows the sermon flow (Intro → Scripture → Points with Observation, Application, Next Steps → Conclusion with Decision, "I Will", Share, Prayer).

My role: I am not the source of sermon content. I do research, take your thoughts, structure outlines, draft and edit documents, and generate the final manuscript. You provide the vision, direction, and final approval.

2. Stages

These 11 stages are fixed and cannot be changed.

Stage 1
Research

I compile the raw materials: scholarly sources, theological commentary, key themes, structure, and quotes.

Stage 2
Points

I ask you questions, offer 5 suggestions, you confirm the points to be used, I build out a summary.

Stage 3
Application/Illustration

I ask how you will apply each point, offer suggestions, you confirm direction, I create a summary document.

Stage 4
Bookends

I ask for your thoughts on the intro and conclusion, you confirm and then write the intro/conclusion.

Stage 5
Outline

Build from Bookends + Points. Includes intro, points (observation, application, next step actions).

Stage 6
First Draft

We send to Claude to write in your voice. Read voice profile, use outline structure, generate manuscript.

Stage 7
Edit 1

Run through Claude Opus 4.6. I present suggestions, you approve/deny each, I incorporate approved edits.

Stage 8
Final Draft

You review, make any edits, and approve.

Stage 9
Edit 2

Run through Claude Opus 4.6 again with different prompt. I present suggestions, you approve/deny each, I incorporate.

Stage 10
Completed

Manuscript is ready. All edits finished. Ready for delivery.

Stage 11
Generate

Create deliverable files: slides/presentation, handouts, any other preaching aids.
